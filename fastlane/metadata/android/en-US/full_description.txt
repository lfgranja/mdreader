Ressoa is a Markdown document reader as an audiobook, 100% offline and air-gapped — no byte of text or audio ever leaves your device.

HIGHLIGHTS
• 100% offline: zero internet permission. No cloud, no telemetry, no tracking.
• Built for Markdown: understands headings, lists, code and technical structure.
• Local neural text-to-speech (Piper pt-BR) via sherpa-onnx — no subscription.
• Read entire directories (chapters with natural sort, gapless).
• Durable persistence: bookmarks in redb with Blake3 hash, smart resume.
• Accessibility: TalkBack 10/10, high contrast, ≥48px targets, RMS visualizer.
• Speed control 0.75x–4.0x with real-time re-anchoring.

PRIVACY
No data is sent to servers. All inference runs on the device's chip. No ads, no trackers.

MONETIZATION
Unlimited free core. One-time license (Pay Once, Own Forever) unlocks multiple voices and directory reading.

COMPATIBILITY
Android 10+ (API 29). Works on phones, tablets and Chromebooks.
